# Compare cloud providers


Claude Desktop on 3P works with Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry. The biggest difference is who processes conversation data. AWS and Google Cloud do this on their own platforms. On Microsoft Foundry, Anthropic does.

This guide is for admins of organizations on Claude Enterprise plans.

This article is part of **[Move from Claude Enterprise to Claude Desktop on a cloud provider](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18426225)**, which defines the terms wave, overlap period, and cutoff date.

**Note:** **[Covered Models](covered-models.md)** have their own retention rules. Their prompts and outputs are **[retained for at least 30 days by default on every platform where they're offered](../20-Models/data-retention-practices-for-covered-models.md)**, and how you enable that retention differs by provider. Decide whether you need these models before you promise users a zero-retention setup.

For setup steps and provider-specific limits, use the setup guides in the first row of the table. Claude Desktop on 3P also supports an **[LLM gateway](https://claude.com/docs/third-party/claude-desktop/gateway)**, **[Amazon Bedrock Mantle](https://claude.com/docs/third-party/claude-desktop/mantle)**, and the **[Claude API](https://claude.com/docs/third-party/claude-desktop/claude-api)**. Most organizations won't need them for this move.
