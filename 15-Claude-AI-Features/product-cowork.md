# Claude Cowork

## The work behind your best work

Claude Cowork completes tasks you can steer from anywhere. Give it a goal, and it works across your files and tools. You come back to polished work for your review.

## Core capabilities

Switch to Claude Cowork when you want to hand off a task. Find it next to Chat.

### Claude Cowork takes on your tasks

Works directly in folders and tools you choose, and runs your task to deliver work for review. No need to copy from a chat or paste into files.

### Say what, not how

Tell Claude what you need, not how. It opens browsers for web tasks and takes over your screen only when it has to.

### See the work as it happens

Claude shows each step: the files it opens, tools it uses, and choices it makes. Follow along from anywhere, and redirect it if you need to.

### Your work follows you

Claude Cowork runs on web, desktop, and mobile and is the same Claude, wherever you work. Start at your desk, pick it up on your phone.

### Claude works when you don’t

Close your laptop, it keeps going. Schedule a task for any cadence, and it runs unattended. You come back to your deck, spreadsheet, or research.

### More than one thing at a time

Big projects are split into chunks that run together. While it drafts, it researches and organizes at the same time. You review polished work.

### A built-in browser for Cowork

When a task requires opening a site, reading a page, or filling a form, Claude opens a browser in the Cowork side panel instead of yours while you keep working.

- No installation needed.
- Separate from your own browser, logins, and tabs.
- Sign in once for every session, or import cookies to stay signed in to your browser (Chrome, Edge, and Firefox on macOS; Firefox on Windows and Linux).

Available in the Cowork desktop app on Pro, Max, and Team plans. Enterprise admins manage it in Organization settings → Cowork.

### Schedule recurring reports, ready for review

Schedule a report once and get it daily, weekly, or monthly. Claude pulls the numbers, compares them to the period before, and builds the deck, so Monday starts with the readout instead of the assembly.

### Reconcile data in spreadsheets

Hand off recurring close work. Point Claude at the folder of regional exports and budget files, and come back to a working spreadsheet with variances flagged and a note on what didn't reconcile.

### Get a first-pass review of every contract at once

Hand off first-pass contract review. Point Claude at the folder of inbound agreements and your playbook, and come back to a memo per contract with each departure it identifies cited to the clause, and the gaps your playbook doesn't cover marked for you for your review.

## FAQ

### What is Claude Cowork?

Cowork is where you hand Claude real work. It runs on desktop, with web and mobile. Claude works in the files and tools you choose and completes multi-step tasks from start to finish. You define the goal. Claude figures out how to get there.

### What’s the difference between Claude Cowork and Chat?

In regular chat, Claude responds to your messages but can't access your files directly. In Cowork, Claude has permission to read, edit, and create files in folders you specify—so it can actually complete tasks rather than just describe how to do them.

### What's the difference between Claude Cowork and Claude Code?

Claude Code is built for software engineering — writing, debugging, and shipping code. It's available as a terminal tool, desktop app, web interface, and mobile app, with integrations for GitHub and popular IDEs like VS Code and JetBrains.

Cowork is built for non-coding knowledge work — research, analysis, document creation, and other multi-step tasks. It uses the same agentic approach as Claude Code.

### Can I assign tasks to Claude from my phone?

Yes. Start a task from your phone, and Claude keeps working in the cloud even when your laptop is closed. Check in, redirect, or review the result from anywhere. Available in Pro, Max, and Team plans automatically. Enterprise plans can opt in.

### What does computer use mean?

Computer use lets Claude interact directly with your screen to open apps, navigate your browser, or run tools. Claude reaches for your connectors and integrations first, falls back to your browser when needed, and only uses your screen as a last resort. This matters because using a direct integration is faster and more precise than navigating through the screen.

When Claude does interact with your screen, it is designed to ask permission before accessing each application. You can see what it's doing and can stop it at any step. Computer use is in research preview for both Cowork and Claude Code.

### What kind of files can Claude Cowork read, create, edit, and analyze?

Cowork can understand the following file types:

- **Documents and text:** Word documents (.docx, .doc), PDF files (.pdf), Plain text (.txt), Markdown (.md), HTML (.html), JSON (.json), CSV (.csv), TSV (.tsv)
- **Spreadsheets:** Excel files (.xlsx, .xls, .xlsm)
- **Presentations:** PowerPoint files (.pptx, .ppt)
- **Images:** Common formats like .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .gif, .svg, .webp
- **Data and config:** YAML (.yaml, .yml), XML (.xml), TOML (.toml)
- **Notebooks:** Jupyter notebooks (.ipynb)
- **Code files:** Pretty much any programming language: Python (.py), JavaScript (.js), TypeScript (.ts), React (.jsx, .tsx), Java, C/C++, Go, Rust, Ruby, PHP, SQL, shell scripts, etc.

You can also use Connectors to view additional files.

### Does Claude Cowork use more of my rate limit?

Yes, agentic tasks consume more capacity than regular chat. Claude coordinates multiple sub-agents and tool calls to complete complex work.

### Can admins control Claude Cowork access?

Yes. Admins can toggle Claude Cowork off for their organization anytime in [Admin Settings (opens in new tab)](http://claude.ai/admin-settings/cowork). Manage Cowork access across teams with role-based access controls.

### Do I need the desktop app?

No. Cowork runs on web and mobile.

The desktop app adds what web and mobile can't reach: the folders and applications on your own computer. If that's where your work lives, start there.
