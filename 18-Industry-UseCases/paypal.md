# Scaling AI for over 12K employees

### Results

- 12,000+: Claude Enterprise monthly active users
- 45 min: Average time saved per sales team member per week

### Background

PayPal operates one of the world’s largest digital payments networks, trusted by hundreds of millions of consumers and merchants. As a globally regulated financial institution, it builds and ships technology that meets a heightened, complex set of compliance requirements.

### Impact

Claude and other tools now enable how many of PayPal’s employees work and the intelligence it delivers to its customers. More than 12,000 PayPal employees now use Claude across product, technology, and sales. Claude Code is among the most-used AI tools on its technology team.

Teams from PayPal’s product organization can now self-serve their product analysis and dashboard creation through Claude. Its product team built a custom MCP server for data queries and retrieval, reducing reliance on engineering and data analysts.

Merchant underwriting, compliance review, and deal cycles are enhanced with Claude, increasing review efficiency and consolidating data that were previously spread across multiple siloed tools. Reviews that once took hours now take a fraction of the time. Since August 2025, PayPal’s sales team has used a custom-built plugin specifically for pipeline management and account risk review, saving an average of 45 minutes per user per week.

Account management teams use AMOS (an Account Management Operating System), an AI-powered analytics platform built on Claude, to get portfolio insights, merchant health diagnostics, and pipeline intelligence in minutes instead of days. This AI-powered analytics platform replaces manual dashboarding with conversational access to live data. AMOS has cut report prep time to 5–10 minutes and surfaces weekly merchant growth signals, some of which were previously undetected. This frees up account managers to spend more time with merchants.

In addition, PayPal is a partner on Anthropic’s AI Fluency for Small Business course, which helps small business staff—whether in customer interactions, back-office management, supply chain operations, or leadership—build practical collaboration skills.
