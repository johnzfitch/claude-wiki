### Account Brief: Lakeshore Industrial Group

*Prepared September 1, 2026 · Account owner: Marcus Nguyen · NYSE: LIG · Renewal: March 2027*


###### Snapshot

Cleveland-based industrial equipment maker and distributor; ~11,400 employees across Fluid Systems, Automation, and Aftermarket Services (FY2025 Form 10-K). FY2025 revenue \$3.2B, up 6% year over year; Automation is the fastest-growing segment at +14% (10-K, p. 38).


###### What changed in the last 90 days

- **Jul 14** — Acquiring Danvers Automation for \$410M; close expected in Q4.
- ‍**Aug 6** — Q2 revenue of \$842M beat guidance; full-year outlook raised to 7–8%.
- ‍**Aug 19 —** Elena Barna named CIO, tasked with consolidating 40+ plant systems (Manufacturing Dive).
- ‍**Risk flag** — Precision-sensor supply constraints cited as a margin risk (Q2 Form 10-Q, Item 1A).


###### Where we stand

- 240 seats live in Aftermarket Services; \$186K ARR; renews March 2027.
- Expansion opportunity: 600 Automation seats, ~\$310K, Stage 3 (CRM · Opp \#4482).
- June 18 QBR: champion Fatima Davis cited 30% faster claims turnaround; asked for a security review.


###### Opportunities and risks

- ‍**Danvers integration:** consolidation is the wedge for the Automation expansion — propose at Q4 close.
- ‍**New CIO:** Barna owns the consolidation budget; no relationship yet.
- ‍**Spending review:** company-wide procurement pause runs through Q3 close. Reg. (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 10(3)


###### Next steps

- Deliver the security review package from the June QBR within two weeks.
- Re-scope Opp \#4482 to add Danvers seats once the acquisition closes.


- Get introduced to Elena Barna via Fatima Davis before Lakeshore's October planning cycle.

## FAQ

### Does Claude connect to Salesforce and HubSpot?

Yes. Claude connects to Salesforce through Salesforce's connector and to HubSpot through the HubSpot connector.

### Can Claude update the CRM after a call?

Yes. /sales:call-summary turns a Gong or Fireflies transcript into the follow-up email, next steps, and structured CRM fields: use case, competition, decision process, and risks. Reps review and send, and the record stays current.

### How does Claude help with pipeline review and sales forecasting?

/sales:pipeline-review pulls the team's open opportunities and flags stalled deals, stage-age outliers, and missing next steps. /sales:forecast produces best, likely, and worst cases with one action per at-risk deal, ready for the forecast call.

### Is customer data secure?

Claude comes with enterprise security and full audit trails, and does not train on your data by default on Team and Enterprise plans.
