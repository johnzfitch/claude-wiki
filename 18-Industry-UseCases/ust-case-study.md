# Strengthening the foundations of global infrastructure

### Results

- 20,000: Associates training on Claude worldwide
- 50–70%: Reduction in chip validation cycle time
- 4 → 2 days: Standard validation turnaround

### Background

UST is a global digital transformation company that builds and runs technology for the world’s banks, telecoms, healthcare organizations, and manufacturers. It works on its clients’ hardest problems at the intersection of software and the physical world: silicon validation, network operations, and claims processing. In this work, reliability and safety have historically depended on manual test scripting and specialist review. UST is putting Claude to work inside these high-stakes engineering environments.

### Impact

UST proved Claude inside its own engineering organization first. On UST’s hardware validation platform, Claude Code, running Opus 4.8, reads chip schematics directly, then generates and runs regression tests to check downstream effects. Claude compares live equipment data against a digital twin—a virtual replica of the hardware—flagging signal-integrity issues before they reach production. UST has cut chip validation cycle time by 50–70%, and its goal is to make its pipeline even faster.

UST now embeds Claude into the systems it builds and runs for clients. In healthcare, UST CarePath uses Claude to turn scattered claims and care data into clear next steps, with human approval before any member-facing recommendation. In telecom, UST IntelliOps uses Claude to predict cellular network failures and cut alert noise for operations teams. In banking, UST FinX embeds Claude-powered agents directly into bank operations: intelligent case handling, servicing automation, and decision support. In each industry, UST pairs Claude with human approval steps and audit controls.

UST is training 20,000 of its engineers, architects, consultants, and industry specialists on Claude worldwide, and building specialized teams to deploy Claude for its clients. With this partnership, UST is bringing Claude to physical AI—intelligence built into the equipment and engineering processes that produce the things people use.
