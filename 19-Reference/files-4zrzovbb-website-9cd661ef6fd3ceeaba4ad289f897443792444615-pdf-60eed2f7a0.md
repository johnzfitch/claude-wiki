Claude Corps Host Organization Application
Preparation Worksheet (not for submission)

This worksheet mirrors the online application so you can gather answers from colleagues before submitting.
Final answers must be entered in the online form at form.typeform.com/to/X0apETWF -- this PDF is for
preparation only and cannot be submitted.


This application takes about 30 minutes. Your progress will autosave.

Before you start, please have the following ready: (1) your organization's EIN, (2) your organization's
Claude.ai (Team/Enterprise) and/or Claude Console Organization ID(s) (find in Admin Settings), (3) name,
title, and email for your leadership sponsor, fellow supervisor, and authorized signer, (4) a sense of the types
of projects you'd want a fellow to work on (it's OK if they're not fully scoped).

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis in the first year of the program, with start dates in October 2026,
January 2027, and August 2027. However, organizations interested in hosting a fellow for the October 2026
cohort must submit their applications by 11:59 pm PT on Friday, July 17, 2026. The first cohort begins
Monday, October 19, 2026.

Questions? Join the host organization webinar on June 17 or read the FAQ. Unfortunately, due to
anticipated volume, we can't respond to individual inquiries during the application window.
