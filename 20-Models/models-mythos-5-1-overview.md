Claude Mythos 5.1 is available only to organizations verified through Anthropic’s verification programs, such as the [Cyber Verification Program(opens in new tab)](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604842). It has the same specifications and pricing as Claude Fable 5.1. To request access, apply to the program that covers your use case.

[See Claude Fable 5.1](models-fable-5-1-overview.md)
